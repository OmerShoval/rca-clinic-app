# Ocean Athlete v2 — build-plan critic

## Desktop verdict
The premise deserves pushback: this is not really a missing-desktop problem, and SPRINT.md §5a already settled the policy — laptop is the coach surface, responsive not two codebases, non-negotiable. The useful question is what that costs, and the answer is much less than it looks.

Three facts change the picture. First, the coach's laptop surface ALREADY EXISTS and already works: coach-dashboard-client.tsx carries a md:w-72 / lg:w-80 two-pane layout and commit 21875ec verified 3 coach pages and 6 coach APIs at BOTH 1440x900 and 375x812. Coach-on-laptop and coach-on-phone are both already satisfied by v1. Second, the design's coach screens — Coach Desk, Session Tagger, Composer, Athlete sheet — are not a desktop layout at all. Coach Desk is a phone launcher: four tiles and a row of roster initials. The design does not omit the coach's desktop; it adds a NEW phone coach surface that nobody has time to build. Third, the student reader is a reading experience, and reading experiences are the single cheapest thing in UI to make responsive, because a book is a measure-constrained column. Clamping the prose to roughly 62–68ch and centering it IS the desktop layout. That is not a compromise; it is what every real reader does at width.

So the cheapest correct answer is: RESPONSIVE FROM THE MOBILE DESIGN, ONE BREAKPOINT (~768px), STUDENT SURFACE ONLY — and leave the coach on the existing v1 dashboard for Aug 31.

Mobile-only is wrong: student-on-laptop is a stated requirement and it is nearly free for a reader, so refusing it buys nothing. A separate desktop layout is clearly wrong: it doubles the surface area of a codebase with 17,558 LOC, no tests, no CI, and ten days to a hard freeze on Aug 28.

Only three things in the mobile design genuinely resist a width clamp, and all three have cheap answers. The Vault shelf with its perspective-rotated spines needs to become a wider shelf or wrap rather than stretch. The floating dock becomes redundant once the page is not thumb-driven, so hide it above the breakpoint. The four bottom sheets (reading settings, Ask, tagger, athlete) are a phone idiom — at width they should become a centered modal or a side panel, which is one shared container decision made once, not four.

The honest risk in the other direction is the Composer. debrief-editor.tsx is 856 lines with literally zero responsive classes, and it is the tool Omer uses to do the actual job. Redesigning it to the v2 phone composer before the clinic is the largest single regression risk in this whole plan, and it buys nothing that the working v1 editor does not already deliver on a laptop at night.

## Recommended order
1. STEP 0 — DEPLOY WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT, BEFORE WRITING ANY NEW CODE. Add COACH_SESSION_SECRET to Vercel, set the missing PIN for noy-bar-lev, make the rca-notes bucket private (it currently holds named clients' voice recordings with public read/write). Deploy the hardening branch. Verifiable: coach logs in on production, all 24 students still authenticate, all 13 debriefs still render. This is finished code sitting undeployed while it blocks everything behind it, and it carries the one failure mode that locks Omer out of his own clinic.
2. STEP 1 — SETTLE FOUR CONTRACTS IN WRITING, NO CODE. (a) Language: Hebrew or English for these 12 athletes, and if Hebrew, which Hebrew serif, since neither design font has a single Hebrew glyph. (b) Theme scope: do the three themes touch v1 screens, and is the design's --accent naming adopted globally or namespaced. (c) What a 'page' is, since one number drives three separate bars. (d) The v1/v2 URL map and which single navigation shell wins. Verifiable: four decisions appended to the SPRINT.md decision log. Every one of these is cheap now and expensive after six screens exist.
3. STEP 2 — INGEST AND SAVE RELIABILITY. This is the actual Aug 31 blocker and it is unblocked right now, because the design does not define bulk ingest and never will. Build a real multi-file dropzone as a NEW surface (video-uploader.tsx is bound to a single slot at maxFiles:1); hoist UploadManagerProvider into app/coach/layout.tsx so navigating dashboard-to-library stops destroying in-flight Cloudflare transfers against one-time upload URLs; add lib/api-fetch.ts with AbortSignal.timeout since there are currently zero timeouts across 62 client fetches; make block saves actually check res.ok instead of returning {ok:true} unconditionally. Verifiable: drop 20 clips at once, navigate away mid-upload and watch them survive, force a 500 and see a visible error. With 12 athletes and roughly 400 clips against 9 in the database today, this is the thing that breaks on day one.
4. STEP 3 — V2 FOUNDATION, ADDITIVE AND SCOPED. A namespaced theme/token layer carrying paper, light and abyss that does NOT touch v1's :root. Literata and IBM Plex Mono wired through next/font with a real Hebrew fallback stack. One additive migration creating reading_progress and bookmarks — no DROP, no destructive ALTER. Verifiable: every v1 screen renders pixel-identical to before, a scratch page cycles all three themes, and a progress row round-trips through the API. This is the last moment where the foundation is cheap.
5. STEP 4 — THE READER, ON REAL DATA, AT BOTH WIDTHS. A new route alongside the existing v1 debrief detail, which stays reachable per the additive rule. Render the 13 real debriefs and 65 real blocks — no fixtures. Everything requiring new authoring (markers, drills, annotations, before/after captions, figure captions, inline highlight ranges, private notes) is omitted or degrades gracefully rather than being stubbed. The desktop layout is the measure clamp plus a centered column; the dock hides above the breakpoint and the sheets become centered modals. Verifiable: open all 13 real debriefs at 390px and 1440px, and confirm progress survives a reload and a second device. This is the one v2 screen with real content behind it.
6. STEP 5 — LIBRARY SHELF AND TOC, REUSING THE STEP 3 STORE. The shelf renders real clinics, the resume card and TOC percentage read the same progress row as the reader footer. Verifiable: all three surfaces show the identical number for the same debrief, and the shelf wraps rather than stretches at 1440px. Stop here if Aug 28 arrives.
7. STEP 6 — AUG 29 DRESS REHEARSAL, EXACTLY AS SPRINT ALREADY SPECIFIES. Real beach, real phone, real cellular: 20 clips, 3 debriefs, hard reload, verify. Aug 30 fixes only what the rehearsal actually broke. With no tests and no CI, this rehearsal is the only real regression suite that exists.

The theme switcher and the font-size stepper are the first things to cut inside the reader, and note the design's own bug: only two elements in the entire 113KB file actually consume var(--fs), so the stepper barely does anything as drawn. ## Open questions for Omer
- LANGUAGE — the blocker above all others. The design is 100% English: zero Hebrew characters, zero dir=rtl, and both bundled fonts (Literata, IBM Plex Mono) declare no Hebrew unicode-range (U+0590-05FF). The shipped app has 108 Hebrew strings and live RTL (lib/language-context.tsx sets dir=rtl). So: do the 12 Indonesia athletes read Hebrew or English? If Hebrew, the entire book aesthetic collapses to a fallback face and someone must pick a Hebrew serif that pairs with Literata — and the reader's mono label gutter, the chevrons, the bookmark clip-path and the vertical writing-mode spine labels all need explicit mirroring decisions, which is structural work, not a CSS flip.
- PAGE COUNT — 'Page 12 of 38' and '9 min left' come from two hard-coded constants (total=38, mins=16) invented by the prototype. Is a page derived from rendered height, word count, or a fixed divisor? This single number feeds three separate consumers (reader footer, TOC row, library resume bar), so it must be defined before any of the three is built.
- READING PROGRESS SEMANTICS — per debrief only, or per block? Does it ever reset? What threshold makes the TOC show a completed tick? Does it sync across devices (a table) or stay local (localStorage)? And on Starlink, how often does a scroll handler write — debounce interval and failure behavior are unspecified.
- THE FRONT-MATTER CHAPTER — the TOC lists 'FM — How to read this book' as a chapter with a read tick. It is not a session debrief and rca.debriefs has no kind/type column. Is it authored once globally, once per clinic, or hard-coded? Where does it live?
- CHAPTER TITLES are two-part in the design ('Day 3 Morning — Back Foot Timing') but wave_label is a single field. Is the second half authored by the coach, or derived from the active goal step? This decides whether the composer needs a new input and whether the TOC can be built at all from the 13 real debriefs.
- VIDEO MARKERS — the reader's signature interaction (three named markers, prose chips that scrub the film) requires someone to author 3 markers per chapter. Nobody has said whether Omer will do that mid-clinic. If he will not, the feature is dead on arrival and the film card should be designed without it.
- THEME SCOPE AND DEFAULT — the design defaults to Paper; the app is committed to dark at :root with color-scheme:dark and an explicit 'no light mode' rule. Do the three themes apply to the 13 existing v1 screens too (which silently restyles shipped work and violates the additive rule) or only inside new v2 routes? And is the choice stored in localStorage or a column?
- TOKEN SEMANTICS COLLISION — in the app --accent is GOLD (#e0b64f) and --primary is TEAL; in the design --accent is TEAL and gold is a separate token. 29 files reference these names. Is the design's naming adopted globally (a rename touching every v1 screen) or namespaced under a v2 prefix? Getting this wrong turns every gold surface in v1 teal.
- WHO AUTHORS THE CONTENT, AND WHEN — habit_logs has 0 rows and translations has 0 rows. The Today card, the 7-day streak strip, the 71% heatmap and the entire Back Home volume all render from those two empty tables. Is anyone writing training programs for 12 athletes before Aug 31? If not, these surfaces ship empty.
- DRILLS — the design shows three independently checkable drills per chapter with a '0 of 3 done' counter, but next_step is one free-text body field. Does Omer want to author three discrete drills per debrief during a live clinic, or is one paragraph the honest unit?
- PRIVATE NOTES — 'MY NOTES · ONLY YOU SEE THIS' is a trust promise. Is it genuinely hidden from the coach? Every rca table currently has allow-all RLS, so today the promise cannot be kept. Making it true is a security task, not a UI task.
- UNREAD STATE — the red 'new chapter' flag and the Ask dot need a last-seen marker that exists nowhere. What marks a chapter as seen, and does opening it count or does finishing it?
- COEXISTENCE URLS — rule 5a.1 says v1 stays reachable. So does the new reader replace /s/[slug]/waves/[debriefId] or live at a new path? Which one do students get linked to, and what happens to the existing bottom-nav when the design's floating dock also renders? Two navs on one screen is the default outcome if this is left late.
- PROSE HIGHLIGHTS — the Mistake paragraph wraps a specific clause in a red-underlined span. debrief_blocks.body is plain text. Is that clause authored (needing rich text or a marks offset store) or is it a design flourish that can be dropped without loss?
- COACH ON PHONE — is the Coach Desk actually needed for Aug 31, or is the real beach workflow 'shoot on the phone camera, upload and write at night on the laptop'? The answer decides whether four coach screens get built or skipped entirely.

## Sequencing risks
- THEME AND TOKEN LAYER MUST COME FIRST, AND MUST BE SCOPED. Two failure modes, opposite directions. Build v2 screens with hard-coded design hexes and retrofit theming later, and every screen gets rewritten. Swap the tokens at :root to match the design and you silently restyle all 13 shipped v1 screens — including flipping --accent from gold to teal across 29 files — which directly violates the additive rule in SPRINT §5a.1. The token layer has to be namespaced and scoped before the first v2 pixel.
- THE READER CANNOT PRECEDE THE PROGRESS STORE. One scroll-derived percentage feeds three separate consumers: the reader footer, the TOC chapter row, and the library resume card. Ship the reader with component-local state and all three get rewritten when the table lands — plus the resume card is the entire point of the library screen.
- NO V2 SCREEN BEFORE THE LANGUAGE DECISION. Every string in the design is English. Hard-code English into six screens and then add Hebrew, and you re-touch all six. Worse, RTL here is structural, not a flip: the reader's fixed mono label gutter, the back chevrons, the bookmark clip-path polygon and the vertical writing-mode spine labels each need a real decision. And no font is chosen yet that can even render Hebrew.
- TOUCHING THE DEBRIEF EDITOR IS THE BIGGEST REGRESSION RISK ON THE BOARD. 856 lines, zero tests, and it is the tool that produces every piece of content the students read. The v2 Composer is a redesign of it. Any rework there lands directly on Aug 31, and SPRINT already records that a previous auth change locked the coach out of his own dashboard because two files held a second copy of a rule.
- BULK INGEST IS DEADLOCKED ON A SPEC THAT DOES NOT EXIST. SPRINT is explicitly holding the top-leverage item — multi-file ingest — waiting for the v2 design to define it. I read the tagger: it describes a per-clip pipeline (clip to movement tag to voice note to chapter draft) and a single plus button. It does NOT define bulk ingest. The design will never unblock this, so the hold is burning calendar for nothing. Meanwhile video-uploader.tsx:224 is still maxFiles:1 and the database holds 9 videos against a clinic that needs roughly 400 in five days.
- THE CLIP VAULT WOULD WAKE DEAD SCHEMA. Its '128 clips, by movement, 6 star best' depends on student_videos.movement_id, which is written by exactly one route and read by nothing. SPRINT names it dead-but-present and says explicitly not to make Aug 31 depend on waking it. Building the Vault does exactly that.
- FONTS LAST MEANS RE-TUNING EVERYTHING. Literata and IBM Plex Mono are bundled in the export as opaque UUID woff2 files, not live Google Fonts links. They must be re-sourced through next/font. Build the reader against fallback metrics and add the real serif afterwards and every line-height, measure and vertical rhythm gets re-tuned — in the one screen where typography IS the product.
- NAVIGATION SHELL DECIDED LATE MEANS RE-PARENTING EVERY SCREEN. v1 has components/bottom-nav.tsx; v2 has a floating dock plus a library home. If both render, students get two navigations. Choosing the shell after the screens exist means touching all of them again.
- THE HARDENING BRANCH IS BUILT BUT NOT DEPLOYED, AND COACH_SESSION_SECRET IS NOT IN VERCEL. If v2 work stacks on top of undeployed security work, the first production deploy carries both at once and doubles the blast radius on the worst possible date. Worse, if that secret is still missing at deploy time, every coach login fails and Omer is locked out of his own dashboard.

WHY IT'S BUILT THIS WAY

The color logic is the clarity feature. Your customers asked for visual clarity on mistake, goal, improvement. Coral, teal, gold answer that without a single label being read. The film-strip spine on the left edge of each card shows where they are in the arc at a glance.

The before/after slider is the emotional peak. "See the improvement to feel better" gets the biggest, most tactile moment on the screen. It's also the clip they'll screen-record and post, which markets the clinic for you.

The "feels like" quote block is your moat. OnForm and CoachNow can annotate video. They can't write "it feels like falling slightly downhill." That italic teal block carries your felt-sense methodology into the product.

Back Home is one correction rendered per environment, not new content. Data model: correction → environment translations (Israel ocean / wave pool / future: any home break). Scales to non-Israeli students with zero redesign.

Ask Omer ships with the SLA visible. A fixed weekly review day makes the product feel premium and keeps your workload bounded. The upgrade button at the bottom is where the continuity revenue lives.

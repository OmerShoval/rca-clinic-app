HOW THE PIECES CONNECT

Mirror rule. Every block in this editor maps 1:1 to a card in the student app. No separate CMS logic to learn — if you can see it as Noy, you can edit it as Omer.

Video slots, not a video tool. Each dashed slot accepts an upload or a link. You keep filming and annotating in CoachNow or your phone; this dashboard is where the finished clip gets placed into the story.

The FPV ⟷ 3rd person pair becomes a slider in the student view, same gold-handle interaction as before/after. Two angles of the same correction is something no coaching app ships natively.

Draft / Live per student. Fill everything during the clinic evenings, publish when ready. The student never sees half-built pages.

Inbox = triage, not chat. Open clip, reply however you want, mark answered. The status is what the student sees in their app.
